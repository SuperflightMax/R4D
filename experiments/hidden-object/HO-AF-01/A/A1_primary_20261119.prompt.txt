Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: original fine-art environment concept painting
Primary request: The backstage wings of an old theatre just after the final performance. The audience and company have left, but the place still holds the warmth and strain of the evening. A complete, beautifully authored wide 16:9 painting, extending naturally to every edge.

Composition and emotional structure: View from within a dark stage wing at roughly human eye height, with an intimate, slightly low oblique perspective. A very close, heavy wine-black velvet curtain occupies an irregular mass at the left and turns softly into the lower foreground. Its weight and folds frame a diagonal passage across scuffed stage boards toward the last pool of warm stage light deeper at the right. The eye should travel around and behind things, not along a row of displayed props. In the middle distance an empty, worn chair carries a recently shed pale eau-de-nil satin costume, its train slipping over the chair and making contact with an old travelling trunk. An unfastened sash and the loosened hem make the gesture feel interrupted a moment ago. A tired bouquet caught against the trunk belongs to this little event rather than a decorative still life. Further back, scenery flats seen on edge, hanging ropes and dark architectural openings imply the unseen rest of the theatre. The source of the remaining light is in the stage beyond, not an all-over room glow. No people.

Light and material: A concentrated amber light grazes the satin and the boards, touches the velvet pile and worn edges, then falls away into deep, legible violet-blue and brown half-tones. Preserve information in the shadows without flattening the darkness. Thin reflected light connects the floor, trunk and cloth. The satin is cool, softly luminous and creased by actual weight; the near velvet is dense, absorbent and softly fringed; timber has rubbed grain, dust at the wall and irregularly worn contact points. Old brass and painted scenic wood support this hierarchy without competing with it. Quiet last-show traces, convincing contact shadows and different distances of overlapping forms carry the story.

Style: sophisticated painterly realism, rich but restrained, confident large shapes followed by selective tactile detail, atmospheric depth, an evocative inhabited place rather than a catalogue or a decorative warehouse. Asymmetry, a compelling whole-frame silhouette, a hush after an event, warmth that is leaving rather than a cheerful golden showroom. Original theatre design; do not recreate any supplied reference or any earlier scene.

Avoid: uniform spacing of objects, front-facing display shelves, evenly lit clutter, black crushed shadows, an empty black strip at the bottom, impossible floating fabric, ornamental detail everywhere at the same intensity, text, captions, logos, borders, labels, interface or overlays. One coherent landscape 16:9 frame, no panels or contact sheet.
