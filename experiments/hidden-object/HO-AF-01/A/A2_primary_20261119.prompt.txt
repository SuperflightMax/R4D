Primary request: A costume gallery above the backstage of an old theatre, moments after the last performance has ended. Create a new original complete 16:9 landscape painting with a distinctly elevated spatial composition.

Composition: The viewer stands on a mezzanine landing, looking obliquely DOWN and ALONG a long wardrobe gallery. The closest railing and thick dark costume shoulders partly cross the foreground; below and to the right there is a real open drop into the dim stage-wing volume. A receding rhythm of garment rails, hooks, suspended costumes, wooden balustrades and thin structural posts leads from the near left into a small deep light at the far end. Make the height and the lower level unmistakable. This is not an eye-level room seen through a curtain.

The central expressive shape is a magnificent but exhausted coral-gold theatrical train: heavy cloth has been laid over the gallery rail after a hurried costume change, with its generous silhouette bending and falling down into the open space below. Give it believable support, drag, tension and weight. Its S-shaped movement connects the near gallery to the space under it. Let the distant gallery disappear behind overlapping fabric and timber.

A warm amber-coral illuminated cloth mass against plum, desaturated indigo and cool shadow is the emotional centre; it must not become an evenly glowing corridor. Hold luminous half-tones in the dark material. The distant warm light is small and atmospheric rather than an enormous overexposed rectangle.
