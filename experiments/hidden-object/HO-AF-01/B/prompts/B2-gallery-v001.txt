Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: original finished environmental concept painting, wide 16:9.
Primary request: The winter garden inside an abandoned grand railway station after heavy rain, seen from an elevated side gallery overlooking a vast overgrown atrium. Paint an expansive, surprising interior world where vigorous nature inhabits a magnificent empty human shell.

Composition B2 — high view from the gallery:
Give the viewer the sensation of standing on a weathered upper gallery, looking obliquely down and across a broad open volume. A close fragment of the iron balustrade and worn stone anchors the near edge; beyond it, the eye drops into the immense atrium. Build the image around large opposing rhythms: long iron spans and curving suspended galleries sweep one way, broad crowns of living foliage rise and spill the other way. The height difference between near gallery, planted floor and distant roof must feel convincing. Make the great open centre and surrounding architecture a bold pictorial shape.

The former terminal's generous atrium has become an indoor forest garden. A great spreading tree and groups of palms and ferns grow through the worn platform floor, with roots and moss physically belonging to stone and old metal. Far below, the curve of disused rails emerges and disappears under growth. Shallow collected rainwater catches a broken reflection of the roof and a few ripples. Climbing vegetation hangs from old gallery edges in long uneven masses, leaving stretches of architecture and air visible. Across the void, distant balconies and stair flights establish human scale and the station's forgotten circulation. The whole place suggests years of gradual reclamation, followed by this fresh passing storm.

Light: broad pearly daylight enters through high glazing and the roof opening after the rain. A pool of clearing sunlight grazes one large canopy and the damp floor; cooler violet-grey and green shadows keep the surrounding volume spacious and quiet. Wet stone and iron reflect differently from translucent leaves. Let foliage form substantial varied crowns, fronds and shadowed interiors rather than flat green patches. The far spans lose contrast in clear moist air; nearby forms remain sensuous and tactile.

Art direction: a beautifully composed original environmental painting in rich illustrative realism, with confident painterly edges, refined colour and an unmistakable sense of huge architecture. Place the life of the garden inside the great stillness of the building. This composition must feel broad, elevated and centred on an atrium, distinct from the low path-level experience.
One complete 16:9 landscape, filled to the lower edge. No people, interface, captions, lettering, labels, decorative frame or graphic overlays.
