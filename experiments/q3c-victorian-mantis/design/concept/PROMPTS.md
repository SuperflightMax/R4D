```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: internal production art direction anchor for a 2D skeletal animation, not final runtime assets.
Create one beautifully composed landscape design sheet showing TWO STATES OF THE SAME exquisitely made Victorian mechanical mantis automaton, on an understated warm grey studio backdrop with a shared ground line. Compact capsule state occupies the left 30%; magnificent fully unfolded mantis occupies the right 65%. Keep the SAME physical scale and identical identifiable component shapes, engravings and colors between the two states: the mantis should be about 2.7 times the height of the squat walking capsule; no resizing of the original abdomen shell. Small clear state labels only: "CAPSULE" and "MANTIS". No decorative border or irrelevant text.

Subject and artistic target: an expensive nineteenth-century collectible clockwork and jewelry automaton, sculptural and powerful, a dramatic insect silhouette with real substance, refined old brass, satin copper pistons, restrained dark petrol vitreous enamel, patina only in recesses, warm amber optical glass. Exquisite engraved details selectively at edges; broad calm material planes and deliberate visual hierarchy. Gallery-quality game concept painting with precise hard-surface construction and rich tactile materials, enough clarity for an asset artist. Three-quarter SIDE view, both states facing right; strong readable silhouette, all parts in frame, clean gaps between appendages, no atmospheric effects obscuring mechanics.

The capsule: compact rounded ovoid metal transport pod, approximately 1.4 wide to 1 high before legs. Two broad overlapping enameled dorsal shell panels form its coherent volume. A large front iris hatch with a hinged brow reveals one charming amber optical eye on a short articulated brass neck. Four short broad segmented WALKING legs, each actually emerges through an open lower side bay with visible hinge bearings and internal seating. Legs are folded robust insect limbs with sculpted tapered armored links and broad articulated feet, never wire sticks with discs. Two folded RAPTorial arms are visibly packed in curved front-side recesses, closed sickle tibia nested against substantial femur, not extra walking limbs. A folded armored neck lies along the upper internal cradle. Visible seams and latches explain where each component is stored. The little pod feels curious and alive.

The mantis: same two large dorsal enamel shell panels now overlap into the substantial horizontal/diagonal ABDOMEN behind the upright front body; never become detached decorative wings. The same strong basal cradle and same FOUR load-bearing walking legs now unfold and spread, supporting a substantial coherent lower body. Feet planted in a broad grounded stance. A long proud armored PROTHORAX unfolds UP AND FORWARD from a compact zig-zag mechanism housed in the capsule, with wide overlapping brass structural links, a continuous spine, visible pivots and copper piston connections. Compact triangular sculpted brass head on the top, same single amber optical eye from the capsule now seated in the head, not a new eye, with the same hinged brow becoming an elegant head crest. Exactly TWO large raptorial forelimbs originate at the front thorax, elbows spread and cocked like a real praying mantis, with thick elongated enameled femurs and imposing curved toothed brass sickle tibiae folded against them. Strong broad flattened forms, wrist pivots and restrained jewelry craftsmanship, powerful catching silhouette, substantial and graceful rather than skinny. Forelimbs and head are the primary visual focus, abdomen the second. Insect anatomy, not a humanoid torso, not a crab, not a robot person. Six limbs total: four walking, two catching. The recognisable capsule shell remains a large cohesive abdomen mass. Every component remains connected to a plausible hinge chain.

Avoid: thin rod legs, small hollow body, chaotic open workshop, dispersed disconnected parts, floating hands or optics, generic sphere robot, enormous empty landscape, mechanical wings, flight form, organic flesh, skull, extra limbs, illustration that merely swaps one complete creature for another. No explosions or dramatic fake glow; beauty comes from the design, materials and silhouette.
```

## 02-transformation-keyposes-v1.png

Входной файл: `01-capsule-mantis-anchor-v1.png` из этой папки; роль: reference identity/materials. Вызов через `referenced_image_paths`, без `num_last_images_to_include`.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: six-panel PRODUCTION TRANSFORMATION KEY POSE SHEET, four intermediate poses and two endpoints, for internal mechanical animation art direction.
Input image 1 is the CHARACTER IDENTITY AND MATERIAL reference. It shows the exact same Victorian brass/copper/dark petrol enamel mantis in capsule and unfolded states. Preserve the head, single amber eye, two large enameled abdomen shell plates, robust armored four walking legs, and two huge sickle raptorial arms. This is a continuity study, not a redesign or alternative concept.

Create an elegant widescreen 3 columns by 2 rows design sheet with six clearly separated panels, matching side-three-quarter camera facing right, all feet on a repeated ground line. Each panel uses EXACTLY the same physical scale for all parts; fit every panel to the final mantis height so the capsule is much shorter. No perspective size cheating. Same beautiful refined engraved brass, copper actuators and restrained dark petrol enamel, warm neutral studio ground. Same gallery-quality hard-surface concept illustration as reference with clear legible connected mechanics. Attractive controlled silhouettes in EVERY intermediate, no loose workshop. Each panel small exact label "K0", "K1", "K2", "K3", "K4", "K5", no other text.

K0: the compact walking capsule, eye peeking from its front iris hatch, four short sturdy folded walking legs emerging through actual open lower bays, two closed raptorial arms nested INSIDE long front-side pockets beneath dorsal shell. Low coherent rounded pod, fully packed folded neck.
K1: lower body BRACES before opening. The same four feet take a slightly wider deliberate support stance; knees flex to lower the heavy capsule slightly, the eye aims forward. The two broad dorsal shell plates unlock and tilt up by ONLY 15 degrees exposing a clear opening above the front cradle. The armored neck still folded low inside. This is a small meaningful intermediate, not already tall mantis.
K2: the two connected links of the prothorax begin unfolding as a compressed Z from the front cradle, raising the SAME head halfway, while the two raptorial arms remain CLOSED, folded tightly against the new chest and still partly inside their actual storage pockets. The abdomen and shell stay a single substantial mass behind. Feet planted. Clear eye/head silhouette, no extra head.
K3: the long armored chest rises further up and forward, the two shell panels rotate BACK DOWN to overlap around the abdomen. Head leads by a slight forward nod; one CLOSED raptorial arm swings up from its storage pocket at its visible shoulder pivot, the other lags about 15 degrees. Walking legs progressively extend to lift the same abdomen, never disappear. Strong coiled diagonal silhouette, all parts connected.
K4: nearly full height. The two substantial raptorial femurs unfold UP AND FORWARD from their now high shoulders, then curved toothed sickle tibiae begin opening in staggered phases; head almost reaches final height. Small angular asymmetry, lower body grounded and broad; original large capsule shell visibly remains the abdomen. No transparent body or missing plates.
K5: the magnificent fully unfolded mantis from the reference, stance proud and weighty, single amber eye in the triangular brass head above long armored prothorax, exactly TWO imposing folded raptorial arms raised, FOUR broad armored walking legs planted. Same large original capsule abdomen, no mass has been added or scaled. Graceful focused catching pose.

Continuity constraints: rigid components never stretch or scale, only visible hinges rotate and explicit short telescoping copper pistons slide. Long chest links fold into Z under shell and unfold without generating new mass. Head/eye never duplicate. All legs retain identity. Shoulder roots remain connected to the same front chest block throughout. Four walking legs plus two catching arms in all states, never eight. Do not reuse identical silhouette in several panels; reveal the ordered mechanically coherent action, not a sequence of unrelated full creatures. No ghosted overlays, blur, trails, smoke, sparks, extra characters, backgrounds, UI, wings, skeletal stick legs or floating parts. Artistic blocking targets, not a claim of engineered collision clearance.
```
